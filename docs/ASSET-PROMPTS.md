# LaunchPad visual assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this project. The final project files are `public/launchpad-hero.png` and `public/launchpad-mark.png`. The mark has a transparent background. The wordmark remains live text for clarity and accessibility. The landing page and login use the new campaign image; the original Verdae reference remains on the client product experience.

## Hero prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: high-end editorial website hero photograph for LaunchPad, a product-launch event management platform by Team Gamma. Create a landscape 3:2 photograph of an intimate, impeccably designed modern product launch venue at blue hour: dark forest-green sculptural stage with a large luminous ivory rectangular portal and a warm amber spotlight, central minimal presentation plinth, curved tiered seating with a few softly silhouetted attendees, refined concrete and brushed brass, an architectural indoor pavilion. The feeling is anticipation before a reveal, warm human hospitality and precise organization. Art-directed cinematic architectural photography, real material grain, gentle haze, soft shadows, subtle film grain, believable perspective, asymmetrical composition with portal on right and spacious charcoal green darkness on left; no skincare products or botanical packaging, no computer screens, no floating UI, no rockets, no spaceship imagery, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Palette near-black pine #142f2a, olive, limestone #f2efe6 and small amber #d5a14d accents. Make this look like a commissioned design studio campaign, not stock conference photography.

## Logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: transparent standalone brand symbol for LaunchPad event-management platform. Design one original crisp geometric monogram combining an uppercase L and P into a single architectural rising doorway: one rounded vertical forest-green pillar, a sculptural rectangular arch forming the P counter, and a small diagonal amber wedge suggesting upward momentum. Restrained Swiss modernist brand identity, flat solid fills, strong elegant silhouette, legible at 32 pixels, balanced negative space, dark forest green #183d34 plus a small muted ochre #d5a14d accent. Center the single mark on a genuinely transparent background, tightly composed with generous but not excessive padding. No wordmark, no letters outside the monogram, no presentation mockup, no drop shadow, no gradients, no lighting, no bevel, no border, no repeated marks, no rocket, no watermark.
